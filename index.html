About the clinic

Physiotherapy that treats the cause, not just the ache

Vitality was founded in 2009 by a small group of physiotherapists who believed recovery should be measured in function, not just pain scores. Today our clinic combines hands-on manual therapy, guided exercise rehabilitation and honest education — so you understand exactly why you're doing what you're doing, and when you'll be done.

Every plan starts with a full movement assessment, not a template. We treat athletes, office workers, new parents and people well into retirement, and we adjust our approach to fit the life you're trying to get back to.
