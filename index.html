About the clinic

Physiotherapy that treats the cause, not just the ache

Meridian was founded by a small group of physiotherapists who believed recovery should be measured in function, not just pain scores. Our clinic combines hands-on manual therapy, guided exercise rehabilitation and honest education — so you understand exactly why you're doing what you're doing, and when you'll be done.

Every plan starts with a full movement assessment, not a template. Based in Block 8, Clifton, we treat athletes, office workers, new parents and people well into retirement, adjusting our approach to fit the life you're trying to get back to.

How treatment works

From first assessment to a plan you can keep

Four stages, one physiotherapist, no guesswork about what happens next.

1

Assessment

A 45-minute movement and history assessment to identify the real source of the problem.

2

Diagnosis & goals

We explain what's going on in plain language and agree on the outcomes that matter to you.

3

Active treatment

Hands-on therapy combined with a progressive exercise plan, reviewed and adjusted each visit.

4

Recovery & maintenance

A discharge plan built to prevent recurrence, with a clear path back if anything flares up.
